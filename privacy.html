Privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Street Meowmi is a personal cat journal operated by ACOTE (A Cat On The Earth). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app or contact support.

Your collection stays on your device

Cat photos, nicknames, map icons, encounter dates, and sighting coordinates are stored in the app’s storage on your device. New collection records are not uploaded to an ACOTE-operated server or published to other users. No Street Meowmi account is required. There is no automatic collection sync between devices.

Camera, photo library, and location

Camera and photo access let you take or select cat photos. Location access shows your position and helps you select an encounter location. The app does not request background location access. You can change permissions in iOS Settings; related features may become unavailable.

On-device image analysis

Apple Vision checks whether an image contains a cat and compares visual features with saved photos. These operations run on your iPhone. Cat photos are not sent to an external AI provider for this analysis. Detection and matching are suggestions and may be inaccurate; you choose which cat to record.

Maps and place-name suggestions

The app uses Google Maps, which communicates with Google to provide map services. Your collection being stored locally does not mean the map works without network communication. Google Maps SDK privacy declarations include diagnostic and usage information, device identifiers, and other data. The included map components also declare user identifiers; some declared data may be linked to a user or device. Google handles this information under its own privacy practices.

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Maps terms: https://www.google.com/help/terms_maps/

When you open nickname suggestions during registration or name editing, the app may send the selected encounter coordinates to Apple’s geocoding service to obtain available street, area, or landmark names. Cat names and photos are not included in this lookup. Without a place-name result, nickname ideas use coat and encounter time locally.

Apple privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Backups and sharing

You can export photos and collection records as a ZIP, including exact encounter coordinates. Street Meowmi does not encrypt these ZIPs. You choose where to save or share them, so keep them private. Services such as iCloud Drive handle saved files under their own privacy practices. Device backups may also include app data, depending on your settings.

Exporting a ZIP alone does not guarantee a backup is stored safely. Save it outside the app and confirm you can access it before resetting your phone, replacing it, or removing the app.

Deletion and retention

You can delete encounters or entire cats in the app. App-managed photos that are no longer referenced are cleaned up when possible. A photo shared by several encounters is retained while another encounter still uses it.

Deletion in the app does not delete copies in your original photo library, exported ZIPs, device backups, or external services. Restoring an older backup can re-add previously deleted records. Removing the app removes its active local storage, subject to copies retained in backups or external services.

Earlier development versions

Some earlier test versions stored photos in Supabase Storage. The current app does not retrieve those online photos or upload new ones there. Older references are retained without fetching the photos; a cat icon is displayed instead. A complete ZIP backup cannot be exported while it contains online-only photo references. Disabling access does not delete copies previously stored on the server. Contact support if you used an earlier test version and need help with those records.

Earlier versions may also leave a local safety copy of the original collection during migration. This is separate from the active journal, and deleting individual records does not remove that safety copy. New installations without legacy data do not create it.

Advertising and purchases

The current build contains no advertising SDK and no ACOTE-operated analytics service. This does not exclude the Google Maps SDK data handling described above.

Purchases are not available in the current general-use build. Purchase development uses Apple’s local StoreKit testing; those test transactions are separate from a live App Store purchase. This policy will be updated before live purchases become available.

Contacting support

If you email streetmeowmi@gmail.com, ACOTE receives your email address, your message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request and investigate the issue you report. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or a full collection backup unless it is necessary and you have agreed to share it. Remove private locations or personal information from screenshots where possible.

As a general rule, support correspondence is deleted approximately one year after the request is resolved, unless it is needed for ongoing support or applicable legal obligations.

Support email is handled through Gmail, which is provided by Google under its privacy practices. You may contact the same address to ask about your support correspondence or request its deletion; some information may need to be retained to resolve an ongoing matter or meet applicable obligations.

This support website

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. This website does not add analytics scripts, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, or a contact form. Clicking an email link opens your email app; information is sent to support when you choose to send the message.

GitHub privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
